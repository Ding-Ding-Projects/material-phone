# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, focus criticism on code and behavior, and make the project usable for people with different abilities, backgrounds, languages, and levels of experience.

Constructive participation includes giving reproducible feedback, acknowledging verification boundaries, respecting accessibility and privacy, preserving upstream attribution, and helping others understand the next safe action.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Reports should use a private maintainer contact route where possible and must not expose a reporter's sensitive information in public records.
